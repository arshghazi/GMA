THE PROBLEM & BOTTLENECK

THE FIRST 72 HOURS & NETWORK COLLAPSE

During major natural disasters (earthquakes, flash floods, landslides, wildfires), conventional infrastructure fails catastrophically.

1. Ground Access Blockades

Destroyed bridges, debris-filled arteries, and active mudflows prevent ground search teams from reaching trapped survivors in the critical Golden 72 Hours.

2. Terrestrial Network Blackout

Cellular towers, fiber optics, and electrical power grids collapse, blinding standard emergency dispatch systems and severing communications.

3. Single-Sensor Blind Spots

Optical-only drones fail in dense dust, concrete smoke, or darkness. Single-sensor platforms cannot reliably distinguish ambient heat from human life.

EDGE SENSING TIER

QUAD-SPECTRUM EDGE AI SENSORS

Single sensors fail under post-disaster dust, fire, or collapse. RescueEye fuses four independent sensory modalities.

1. FLIR Thermal IR

MLX90640 32x24 array detects 30°C–40°C human body heat through concrete crevices, darkness, and dust.

2. 4K RGB YOLOv8

YOLOv8n-SAR models identify trapped limbs, faces, occluded bodies, and waving distress gestures at 30 FPS.

3. 4-Mic DSP Acoustic

ReSpeaker array with 200–800Hz rotor noise notch filter spots cries for help ("HELP", "SAVE ME", "SOS").

4. UWB Radar Vitals

Ultra-Wideband sub-debris radar detects microscopic chest-wall movement associated with respiration (14 bpm).

MATHEMATICS & AI ENGINE

BAYESIAN MULTI-MODAL SENSOR FUSION

Computes an actionable survivor confidence score ($C$) to eliminate false alarms and automate triage dispatch.

Calibrated Fusion Formula
C = (0.35 × Thermal) + (0.25 × RGB) + (0.25 × Voice) + (0.15 × Radar)
  • Thermal Weight (35%): Unmatched for occluded debris crevices.
  • RGB Weight (25%): Precise shape and posture validation.
  • Voice Weight (25%): Direct active survivor confirmation.
  • UWB Radar (15%): Biological life sign verification.
Triage Classification Matrix
  • CRITICAL ($C \ge 85\%$): Immediate ground extraction unit dispatch; drop GPS beacon.
  • HIGH ($70\% \le C < 85\%$): Hover & deep acoustic scan; dispatch secondary search unit.
  • MODERATE ($50\% \le C < 70\%$): Mark on tactical GIS map for verification.
  • LOW ($C < 50\%$): Ambient artifact; log and continue search.
